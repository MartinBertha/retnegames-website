Tiny Trader

You are a halfling in a small rowboat with a big dream: to build a trading empire. Buy low, sell high, and adapt to seasonal changes in a procedurally generated fantasy world inhabited by humans, elves, dwarves, and orcs. Complete quests and supply goods in demand to build your reputation with each race and unlock lucrative luxury goods. Upgrade your fleet, hire captains, and establish profitable trade routes between cities as you grow from humble beginnings into a thriving trading empire.
